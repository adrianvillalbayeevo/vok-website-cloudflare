# VOK — Custom Software Platforms

## 1. Project Description
**Product positioning:** VOK builds custom software platforms around the way a business actually operates. Not a generic SaaS. Not a traditional development agency. A boutique product studio that studies operations, workflows, pain points, rules, payments, users and exceptions — then builds the client's own software around them.

**Target users:** Sports clubs and academies (owner-led youth sports organizations, 200–1,000 athletes) initially, with expandable scope beyond sports.

**Core value:** Your way. Your platform. Custom software finally made accessible through compact teams, agentic AI-assisted development, and rapid iteration.

## 2. Page Structure
- `/` — Home (hero, case studies, problem statement, your way section, AI section)
- `/platform` — Platform process (discover, map, design, build, your platform)
- `/work` — Portfolio / case studies (Triumph, Cedros, Legacy Falcons)
- `/about` — About VOK (team, methodology, agentic development)
- `/book-demo` — Book a demo form
